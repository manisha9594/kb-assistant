# Northwind Logistics Travel and Expense Policy

## Approval
All business travel must be approved in Concur by your manager before booking. Trips outside North America also require director approval.

## Flights
Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance when possible.

## Hotels
The nightly hotel limit is $250 in standard cities and $350 in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, Boston, London).

## Meals
The daily meal allowance while traveling is $75. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of an approved client entertainment event.

## Submitting expenses
Submit expense reports in Concur within 30 days of the expense, with itemized receipts for any expense over $25. Reports submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed.
